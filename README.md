Space Shooter 2d
A 2D space shooter game made with Unity 6 and C#. Destroy enemy ships, dodge their fire, and survive for a high score.
Control your spaceship and shoot down enemy ships to score points.
Enemies spawn continuously and fire back at you.
You start with 3 lives. Getting hit costs a life. When lives reach zero, it's game over.
Survive as long as you can. Your score and survival time are shown on the HUD.
Explosion effects, scrolling stars and passing planets create a lively space atmosphere
